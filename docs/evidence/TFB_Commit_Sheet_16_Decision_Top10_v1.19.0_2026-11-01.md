## S1 - WHY
- A qualified seated name must not read as "missed" and lose its plan because the backend's research pass deferred it from the sized list on that day; it should keep the status the grid gives it and be offered to the existing replay, which decides.
- A grace seat must show today's numbers, and say when its source row is investable, instead of a stale stash price and blanks.
- The owner's rule must be readable on the board, seat by seat, so a GO / NO-GO needs no hand check.

## S6 - Known limits / deliberate cuts
- **The replay can size a held seat only if its row is in the frozen snapshot.** The backend's `board_funding_snapshot.rows` carries only the research picks (`opportunity_builder` 7841: `[p["cand"]["_board_source_row"] for p in picks]`), and a research-stage deferral (sector / market cap, lot or minimum-ticket floor, held target, issuer dedup, price cross-check) is exactly what keeps a qualified name out of the sized list. Under `enforce` such a seat is offered (`board_funding_symbols`) but the replay cannot size it; it then reads `ACTIVE (day n)` + `Research seat -- no executable allocation today` instead of `GRACE (0/n missed)` - the status and the day count are kept, the plan is not. The sizing half of R2.0 needs a backend change (a snapshot that carries every INVEST row inside the rank buffer); the harness proves the GAS half with a stub that sizes the offered symbols.
- The hold applies only under the `qualified` confirmation basis (`TFB_T10_CONFIRM_BASIS` default), to confirmed members (not unconfirmed fast-track seats), with `co === 0` (a same-day re-run after a miss outside the buffer keeps GRACE n/3), and never to an outage-paused seat.
- Live fill replaces a ghost's price with the row's price and fills only blank Rel / DQ / FX / Price SAR; a src-present GRACE or FAST-TRACK seat keeps its own raw numbers (they are today's). Engine ROI and the confidence band are not filled (not asked; the owner-rule cell then reads `conf unknown` / `ROI unknown` on a stash ghost). On a NOT_ACTIONABLE feed the Advisor Note cell is still the v1.10.0 withhold text (the numbers stay).
- The `Owner Rule` column is written after Advisor Note (column 31; 33 when the vintage columns are enforced); the first armed render inserts the column on the live sheet once. `_Selection_Log` does not carry it (its schema is unchanged).
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` was not edited (out of the named files); the pytest wrapper collects the harness in the whole-suite job. Adding `node tests/test_dt10_v1190_hold_live_rule.js` to the native Apps Script step is a one-line follow-up.
- Nothing here changes R2.1-R2.4 (hysteresis, incumbency margin, smoothed ranking, fixed-time logging): shadow items, separate PRs.
