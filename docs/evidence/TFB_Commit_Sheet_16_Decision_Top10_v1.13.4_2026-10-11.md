## Known limits / deliberate cuts
- The News gate stays NOT EVALUABLE by design until the owner adopts news→action coupling in the backend (routes v4.10.0 registered hypothesis); the Sector gate has no wired input in the 115-column contract. The disclosure states this; it does not fix it. - Enforce→off asymmetry: off never writes or deletes, so L15:M16 keep their last text and `_T10_Candidates` keeps its last rows (stamped with its run id) until the operator clears them.
