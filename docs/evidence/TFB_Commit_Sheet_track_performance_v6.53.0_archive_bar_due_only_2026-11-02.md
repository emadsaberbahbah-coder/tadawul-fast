## What the audit writes today for a NOT-due active record (established before the gate was built)
The loop (`audit_active_records`, the `for r in active` body) does, for a record with `now < target`: when its symbol was priced this pass, `Current Price` and `Unrealized ROI` from the fresh quote and `Last Updated = now`; when its symbol was NOT priced (breaker, budget, no witnessed quote), `Last Updated = now` only. Status, Realized ROI, Outcome and Notes (no receipt) are never written for a not-due record either way. Under `TRACK_AUDIT_DUE_ONLY=1` a not-due symbol is simply not in the priced set, so its records take exactly the second path: `Last Updated = now`, every other cell untouched, no receipt. D2 proves it row for row against a gate-off pass in which the same symbols were unpriced. A symbol with one due and one not-due record is priced (its not-due record takes the fresh mark as today); a record whose target cannot be parsed is not due (it cannot mature) and keeps today's `PENDING outcome_target_time_unknown` receipt without evidence.
