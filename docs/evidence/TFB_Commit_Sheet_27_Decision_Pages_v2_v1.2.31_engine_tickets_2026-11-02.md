### S2.3 Review finding fixed on the PR (Codex P1, comment 4440547245)
The capture mapping (`dp2IbkrOverlayCapture_`, the step-3 line) took an IBKR order's **total** quantity as `qty`, and `dp2BracketFor_` sums `qty` into `stopQty` - the coverage behind the lot allocation, the `STOP COVERS … place a stop for …` advice and the partial-coverage order status. A partly filled stop (e.g. 100 shares with 20 remaining) therefore read as full protection. Verified on the harness (v1.2.30: a 14.56 stop with 100 of 174 remaining reads `working since …`, full). Fix: `dp2CoverQty_` - coverage = the remaining quantity, capped at the order total and floored at 0, when the capture carries it; the total otherwise (and for `_IBKR_Orders` tab rows, unchanged); the total is kept on the row as `total`. Behind `DP2_COHORT_LEGACY` like everything else here, so the switch still restores the 1.2.30 build byte for byte. Effect: `working since 2026-10-26T06:23:34Z — stop covers 100 of the position's 174 sh (partial)` and `Brackets working on every equity lot 0 of 1 · verify`; never more protection than before.
