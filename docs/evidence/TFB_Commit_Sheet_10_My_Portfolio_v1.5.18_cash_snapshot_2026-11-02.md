The 11 Oct audit measured the seven v1.0.1 columns on the live tab; the reconciliation contract (`docs/portfolio_reconciliation_contract.md`, "Operator-maintained cash export template") asks for exactly these six names appended to the right of `Note`, existing rows untouched. The self-test line reports the live verdict before anything is armed (S7 step 2).
- **No lock around the same-day check.** The watcher already holds the script lock while it renders, so the writer takes none; two renders inside the same second could both pass the scan and append twice (the audits report a duplicate date as WARN, never as certified cash).
- **One cash currency per row.** A capture with USD and SAR cash is withheld from the tab (`several cash currencies`) while the request still carries the summed certified cash; a consolidated SAR row is a later decision.
- **Presentation only.** The last-known token changes no control, cell or packet; the Top_10 panel cell and Portfolio_Decision_v2 are not written here (a later item can point the panel at the newest row).
- The writer runs only under `PD_IBKR_EVIDENCE` observe/enforce (there is no plan otherwise) and only when a render happens inside 14 minutes of a capture - the watcher window (`PD_IBKR_WATCH_WINDOW`, default 08:30-10:30 Riyadh) and the capture calendar decide which capture becomes the day's row.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` was not edited (out of the named files); the pytest wrapper collects the harness in the whole-suite job. Adding `node tests/test_my_portfolio_v1518_cash_snapshot.js` to the native Apps Script step is a one-line follow-up.
