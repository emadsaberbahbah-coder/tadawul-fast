# Portfolio blank-fee witness — 2026-10-08

The first 6.64.9 portfolio rebuild stopped before fetching, clearing or writing because an active native Buy Fees cell was blank. The prior page remained intact. The same bounded ledger snapshot contained one unique native Cost Basis equal exactly to Shares × Buy Price for that row; explicit fee inputs on the other active rows reconciled to their native totals. This artifact contains no financial amounts, holding identities, workbook identifiers or credentials.

Synchronizer 6.64.10 permits a blank fee only when exact Buy Price, Shares and Buy Fees headers, a unique native Cost Basis and the existing validated currency/cohort establish that value-bound zero ledger fee component. The raw blank and `buy_fees_basis=native_cost_basis_zero_delta` remain in the private frozen snapshot. This receipt describes the ledger, and does not certify broker commissions. Cost Basis cannot replace the required unit price or quantity. No tolerance, provider fallback or currency conversion is used to infer a zero fee.

Unknown/nonblank fee tokens, nonfinite or negative fees, an absent/duplicate/SAR-only native total, mismatched totals and quantity/unit-cost aliases outside this narrow witness preserve the existing fail-closed behavior. Explicit numeric fee inputs and fee-absent legacy ledgers retain their previous behavior. The same snapshot supplies the actual acquisition cohort and final holdings injection, preventing a ledger update during acquisition or later restoration from adding fees twice.

The new 59-case suite uses synthetic inputs and the real 122-column producer, reader, request, portfolio runner, restoration, injection and final guard. It covers None/empty/whitespace blanks, exact equality including one-ULP rejection, malformed and ambiguous inputs, same-snapshot updates, currency disagreement, partial acquisition provenance, idempotent repricing and fee/doubled-fee corruption. The affected portfolio/acquisition/label/fee suites plus manifest pins passed **245 tests** in the exact Python 3.11 lean environment. The new suite is included in blocking lean CI.
