# Final cash-floor observations — native cockpit 1.12.2

The native finalizer could leave a cash-floor observation describing research-sized seats after the final displayed board withheld every ticket. Monetary amounts were already zero; the observation denominator still came from the earlier research or allocation payload. The same gap could occur when the feed or rollout control changed after a successful replay.

The finalizer now observes only its final executable tickets in board order. It mirrors the unchanged Python backend's `_cash_floor_finalize`: `seats_sized` is the final count; observe mode rounds each displayed `suggested_sar` with Python's half-even rule, walks cumulative size against `deployable_post_sar + 0.5`, and rounds the accumulated would-lose-funding SAR with the same rule. Only `seats_sized`, `would_unfund_seats`, `would_unfund_sar` and the matching existing observe alert's count/tail change.

Backend policy heads and pre/post-budget wording remain byte-for-byte intact. Unsupported observe wording receives an explicit final-board observation rather than retaining stale seat counts. Missing cash-floor metadata or alerts never create a policy or an alert. Non-observe modes retain their existing loss fields and alerts while updating the final sizing denominator. Cash-floor percentage, NAV, reserves, pre/post deployable budgets, mode, allocation, eligibility, feed gates, stability and all ticket/KPI money remain unchanged.

They cover the actual zero-eligible short circuit, mixed final tickets, a successful replay later withheld at render, rollout and feed changes, replay failures, ordering/tolerance/rounding boundaries, idempotence, missing metadata/alerts and unchanged unrelated narratives.
