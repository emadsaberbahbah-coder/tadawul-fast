It carries a price, equity-style synthetic forecasts and an equity SELL
recommendation. Its final action is already `DO_NOT_INVEST`, but its
instrument identity and analytical presentation are still unsuitable.

The issuer's [23 July 2026 Form 8-K](https://investors.dime.com/node/25311/html)
registers **DCBG** as **9.000% Junior Subordinated Notes, $25 par value** on
the New York Stock Exchange. The same registered-securities table separately
identifies common stock under **DCOM**. Therefore DCBG must not receive a
common-equity caption, valuation or forecast merely because its provider
symbol has a US equity-like suffix. This link is a Form 8-K, not a Form 8-A.
The filing establishes the note/common-stock distinction; it does not, by
itself, certify a current quote, current redemption status or provider mapping.

The issuer's [2 April 2026 transfer notice](https://investors.dime.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dime-transfer-new-york-stock-exchange)
separately identifies DCBG as the fixed-to-floating subordinated notes due
2034, with NYSE trading beginning 7 April. Both issuer bodies were retrieved
in this review. Neither source is used here to assert call terms or certify
the provider's current instrument mapping. The implementing authority registry
must retain its own retrieval receipts and finite review validity.

`2026-10-22T23:30:37.440565Z` (**02:30:37 Riyadh**); its acquisition warning

Candidate scope is a narrow exact-authority instrument guard: verify current
