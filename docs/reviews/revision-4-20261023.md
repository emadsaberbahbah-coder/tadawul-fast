## Confirmed defect, owner and correction

The [provider's official glossary](https://eodhd.com/financial-academy/financial-faq/fundamentals-glossary-common-stock)
separates reported diluted TTM EPS (`EarningsShare`, `DilutedEpsTTM`) from
current-fiscal-year estimated EPS (`EPSEstimateCurrentYear`). Two owning paths
violated this distinction:

* EODHD fundamentals selected the estimate as the fallback `eps_ttm`.
* Engine canonicalization accepted `epsCurrentYear` as a trailing EPS alias.

The candidate keeps estimated EPS in `eps_estimate_current_year`, outside the
trailing field and scoring vocabulary. Invalid first reported fields can fall
through to a valid reported TTM alias; actual zero/negative earnings survive.

Independent review found that removal of the estimate exposed an existing
net-income/current-shares proxy: one to three available quarters could become
TTM EPS, and current shares are not weighted diluted-period shares. That proxy
is now diagnostic `eps_net_income_current_shares`, never `eps_ttm`, even with
four records. It supplies no new reported EPS or valuation eligibility.

`_eps_basis` retains the selected source field, observed value, period basis,
supplier update date, reported period end when applicable, and independent
collection timestamp. Missing dates remain missing; none certifies current
quote freshness or financial comparability. Landed-value handling prevents
source proof from following a losing fill-only scalar; live overwrite,
snapshot merge and coherence clearing discard stale proof. LKG restores
currently have no persisted per-field EPS receipt and do not invent one.

Provider fundamentals cache keys and Redis fundamentals-LKG namespace are
versioned to exclude old estimated-TTM entries. Old keys expire naturally;
history is not migrated, rewritten or deleted. This causes a cold refill and
temporarily reduces fallback continuity. Existing throttles, quota controls,
publication gates, freshness tolerances and compliance rules remain intact.

`core/data_engine_v2.py` 5.157.2, small `core/eps_basis.py`, deployment manifest,
and an explicit required-CI regression entry. Open PRs were inspected before
